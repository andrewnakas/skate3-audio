# One step per line: <duration_ms> [buttons] [lx= ly= rx= ry= lt= rt=]
#   buttons: a b x y lb rb l3 r3 start back up down left right
#   sticks -100..100 with up and right positive; triggers 0..100
#   A step holds exactly that pad state for its duration; anything unnamed is neutral.

#   push A; ollie = right stick down then flick up; forced bail = hold LB+RB, press Y
