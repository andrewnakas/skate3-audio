## The record body, shared by both passes

Per record, at cursor `c`:

```
keep_a = *(u32*)(c+24); keep_b = *(u32*)(c+28);
*(u32*)(c+40) = 0;

*(u32*)(c+36) = keep_a;
```

That is a partial rotation, not a clear: `+24`/`+28` are copied down to `+36`/`+56`. The `stbu`
writing at `c+60` means the byte it clears is at offset 0 of the following record, which is why
the port keeps the cursor form rather than inventing a record base.

**The one difference between the two passes**: the first adds 1 to the word it read from the
`+384` array before storing it at `+44`; the second stores the word from `+1068` raw.
